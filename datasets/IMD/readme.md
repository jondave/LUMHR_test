# English Indices of Deprivation (IMD)

The English Indices of Deprivation (IoD), including the Index of Multiple Deprivation (IMD), published by the Ministry of Housing, Communities & Local Government (MHCLG), measuring relative deprivation across Lower Layer Super Output Areas (LSOAs) in England.

[Ministry of Housing, Communities & Local Government - English Indices of Deprivation 2019](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/english-indices-of-deprivation-2019)
