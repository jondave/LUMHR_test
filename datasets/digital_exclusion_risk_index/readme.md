# Digital Exclusion Risk Index (DERI v1.6)

The Digital Exclusion Risk Index (DERI) developed by the Greater Manchester Combined Authority (GMCA) / Greater Manchester Open Data Alliance (GM ODA) calculates risk scores (0–10) across demography, deprivation, and broadband infrastructure indicators for Lower Layer Super Output Areas (LSOAs).

[Greater Manchester Combined Authority - Digital Exclusion Risk Index (GitHub)](https://github.com/GreaterManchesterODA/Digital-Exclusion-Risk-Index)
