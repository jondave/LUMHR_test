# Car or Van Availability (Census 2021 TS045)

Office for National Statistics (ONS) Census 2021 dataset TS045 providing household counts by number of available cars or vans (0, 1, 2, 3+) for all 2021 Lower Layer Super Output Areas (LSOAs) in England and Wales.

[Office for National Statistics (ONS) - Car or van availability TS045](https://www.ons.gov.uk/datasets/TS045/editions/2021/versions/4)
